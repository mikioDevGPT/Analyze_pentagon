# App Review notes

Pentagon Eats is a no-login restaurant guide for Pentagon City and nearby
commercial areas in Arlington, Virginia.

- Restaurant information is based on official business and shopping-center pages.
- Open/closed status is calculated from published opening hours.
- The three-hour planner compares dining options by time of day.
- The app does not request device location.
- Version 1.0 has no ads, analytics, tracking, accounts, purchases, or user content.
- Every restaurant card links to official information for current-hour checks.

All three tabs are available without credentials.
