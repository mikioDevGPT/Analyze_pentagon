## Description

Pentagon Eats is a simple restaurant guide for Pentagon City and nearby
commercial areas in Arlington, Virginia.

Features:

- Open and closed status based on published business hours
- Cuisine and price categories
- Public address or shopping-center location
- A three-hour dining planner
- Direct links to official restaurant information
- No account, device location, advertising, or tracking

Business hours may change for holidays or temporary closures. Check the linked
official page before visiting.
